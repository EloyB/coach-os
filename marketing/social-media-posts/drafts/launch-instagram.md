## Notes

- **Pilot offer is the conversion mechanism here.** Without it this is "we launched a website" which converts ~nothing. With it, you're trading short-term revenue for product feedback + future case studies.
- **Be ready to handle DMs.** A launch post with a real offer should generate inbound. Have a quick reply template ready ("Bedankt voor je interesse — kunnen we 15 min bellen om te zien of jullie club een goeie match is voor de pilot?").
- **Define "real use".** Internally know what counts as a successful pilot before the first DM lands. Otherwise you'll get clubs that sign up, never use it, and you have nothing to show.
